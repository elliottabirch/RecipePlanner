<objective>
Implement the cheap check-off recompute: given the GA's fixed order and actual completion timestamps, re-time the schedule in one forward pass without re-evolving. This is the mechanism that makes check-off feel instant on the tablet (PREP-04/AC5) and keeps the now/next sequence stable (D-01a.3). Turns green retime.test.ts from Plan 02.

Purpose: Re-running the GA on every check-off would let the order jump around mid-cook — explicitly forbidden. Order is authoritative, the clock adapts.
Output: `lib/scheduler/retime.ts`.
</objective>
